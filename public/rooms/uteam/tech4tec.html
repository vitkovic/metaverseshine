teach4TEC Approach

U-TEAM Methodology | Pedagogical-Psychological Integration in Technical Education

Purpose and Scope

teach4TEC is the U-TEAM direction focused on technical education. It introduces pedagogical, psychological and teaching-methodology principles into engineering and other technical disciplines while preserving disciplinary rigor.

The approach focuses on how students encounter, process, practise and develop technical knowledge.

Key Methodological Principles

  • Preservation of technical validity and engineering standards.
  • Management of cognitive complexity.
  • Scaffolding and progressive autonomy.
  • Structured feedback and reflection.
  • Evidence-based improvement of teaching activities.

Application Areas

Engineering courses, CAD/CAE modelling, simulation, programming, laboratory work, design projects and other technical learning environments.

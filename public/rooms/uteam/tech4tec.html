teach4TEC Approach

SHINE Erasmus+ | U-TEAM Methodology

Introduction

teach4TEC is the U-TEAM methodological direction focused on technical education. It integrates pedagogical and psychological principles into engineering and technology teaching while preserving disciplinary requirements.

Objectives

  • Improve organisation of complex technical knowledge.
  • Support active learning and student autonomy.
  • Enable structured feedback and reflection.
  • Improve teaching practice through evidence-based approaches.

Application Areas

Engineering education, CAD/CAE, simulation, programming, laboratories and design projects.

teach4TEC Approach

U-TEAM methodological content developed within SHINE.

1. What is teach4TEC?

teach4TEC applies pedagogical-psychological principles and teaching approaches to technical and engineering education while preserving disciplinary standards.

2. Core principles

  • Scaffolding
  • Cognitive demand management
  • Structured collaboration
  • Feedback and reflection
  • Progressive transfer of responsibility

3. Application areas

Engineering education, CAD/CAE modelling, simulation, laboratory work and process planning.

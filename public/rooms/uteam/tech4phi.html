teach4PHI Approach

U-TEAM Methodology | STEM/STEAM Integration in Philology and Humanistic Education

Purpose and Scope

teach4PHI transfers selected STEM/STEAM principles into philology and humanistic education. The aim is not to replace interpretation with technology, but to expand the ways students investigate, create and communicate knowledge.

Key Methodological Principles

  • Inquiry and research-question formation.
  • Evidence-based analysis.
  • Data and digital literacy.
  • Design, creation and iteration.
  • Technology-supported investigation.

Application Areas

Literature, cultural studies, digital humanities, language learning and communication-oriented projects.

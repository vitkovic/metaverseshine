1. What is teach4PHI?

teach4PHI enriches humanities education through inquiry, evidence use, design approaches and digital practices while preserving disciplinary interpretation and standards.

2. Core Principles

Inquiry

Learning starts from questions, investigation and structured exploration.

Evidence Use

Students work with systematic evidence to support interpretation and conclusions.

Iteration

Design, creation, feedback and revision become part of the learning process.

3. Application Areas

Digital humanities, literature, cultural studies, philology, linguistics and communication-oriented projects.

teach4PHI Approach

teach4PHI transfers STEM/STEAM principles into humanities, philology and related social-science learning contexts.

1. What is teach4PHI?

teach4PHI is the U-TEAM methodological direction that transfers selected STEM/STEAM principles and practices into humanities, philology and related social-science learning contexts.

The approach enriches humanistic learning through inquiry, systematic evidence use, design approaches and digital practices while preserving interpretation, context and disciplinary standards.

2. Why teach4PHI?

Humanities education increasingly benefits from structured investigation, digital tools and creative production. teach4PHI provides a way to introduce these mechanisms without replacing humanistic judgement.

The goal is to strengthen analysis, argumentation, evidence use and reflective learning.

3. Core teach4PHI Principles

Inquiry-based learning

Students formulate questions, investigate sources and construct knowledge through exploration.

Evidence and Data Literacy

Systematic evidence supports interpretation, comparison and critical evaluation.

Design Thinking and Iteration

Creation, feedback and revision become part of the learning process.

4. Application Cases

Digital Humanities Inquiry

Combination of close reading, systematic evidence and digital investigation while keeping humanistic interpretation central.

Cultural Studies through Project Learning

Application of problem framing, design thinking, prototyping and iterative revision.

Philology and Linguistics through Data-driven Inquiry

Use of systematic textual evidence to support and refine disciplinary interpretation.

5. From Methodology to Practice

teach4PHI connects methodological principles with concrete teaching scenarios, activities, resources and assessment approaches.
